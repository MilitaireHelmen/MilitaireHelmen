The Netherlands Beret Regiment Bevoorrading & Transport

The Netherlands Beret Regiment Bevoorrading & Transport
 
Beret is made by the company Hassing BV
NSN 8405-17-117-5516
O.nr.872.2665.1911.11.05
 
Bevoorrading & Transport = Supply & Transport
 
Some information about NATO Stock Number
I have learned something about NSN, some more information: NATO Supply Classification Groups
8405 = Outerwear, Men's
17 =  The Netherlands
 
The yellow backgrond has been in use from 2000 till 2009

Belgian Beret "Chasseurs à Cheval / 2de Jagers te Paard"

Belgian Beret "Chasseurs à Cheval /  2de Jagers te Paard"
 
Chasseurs à Cheval = Jagers te Paard = Hunters on Horse read more at http://www.legerdienst.be/index.html
and 2de Jagers te Paard, is a Cavalry regiment.
 
Moto: "Reigersvliet"
The Battle of the Reigersvliet was a spring offensive with which the German troops attempted to occupy the Belgian outposts in order to be able to transfer to the sea ports of Dunkirk, Calais and Boulogne.
 
In 2004 it is decided to combine 2de Jagers te Paard and 4de Jagers te Paard into one battalion. 
more at: http://www.dewits.be/KVRVlBt/t1.html

Belgium Beret Logistiek gold

Belgium Beret Logistiek gold
 
A.B.L. = Armée Belge / Belgisch Leger
= Army Belgium
 
Blue Beret made by preta in the year 1990.
PRETA according to Google still exists as manufacturer of professional clothing.
"Pre"vost-"Ta"vernier BVBA;
Oude Tieltstraat 118 A Meulebeke .
 
From this forum https://www.ablhistoryforum.be/viewtopic.php?f=39&t=819&p=49819#p49819
I have learned all Logistics Bataljons are wearing the same beret and emblem.
The Blue beret can have this insignia in 3 different colors:
copper = troops
silver    = nco's non commissioned officers (onderofficieren)
gold = officers
 
mitin is the product they used to protect the fabric against the moth.
