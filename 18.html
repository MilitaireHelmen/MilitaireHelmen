Baretembleem 2 Technische Staf

 

The Netherlands Technische Staf

het Dienstvak van de Technische Staf = the Service Section of the Technical Staff

Moto: Arte Pugnantibus Adsum:
Arte pugnatibus adsum" is a Latin expression and can be translated with:
The science is present with the combatants, or (as established by the Army order):
With professionalism I assist the warriors.
Artes = art, science
Pugnantes = the warriors
Adsum = be present, be there

Baretembleem 6 "Opleidingscentrum Officieren van Speciale Diensten"

 

The Netherlands "Opleidingscentrum Officieren van Speciale Diensten"


The Training Center for officers of special services (OCOSD) was
a Dutch officer training for the Royal Netherlands Army that existed from 1974 to 1996.
Until the establishment of the OCOSD, the officer training was only accessible with a
VWO diploma. This training is provided by the KMA.
Yet there was a need to train officers with a different prior education.
The OCOSD was established for this purpose on 2 October 1974.
The OCOSD was accessible with;
HAVO diploma
hts diploma
Reserve officers
Professional petty officers

Moto: Suscipere Et Finire = Accept and end

https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baretembleem

"OCIO"

At the OCIO they learn all about their live in the Military.
After their initial training at the OCIO the recruits will go to the "Functionele Opleiding" where they learn the skills for the job they will full fill in the army.

Baretembleem 15 Regiment Limburgse Jagers

 


https://www.limburgsejagers.nl/content/

By far the most well-known ready part of the Regiment Limburgse Jagers was founded in 1957, the 42nd Panzerinfanterie Battalion (42 PainfBat), which was nicknamed 42nd Battalion Limburgse Jagers (42 BLJ), where many professionals, former shortage (KVV / TS / BBT) and conscripted personnel has spent his service.
42 PainfBat was stationed from 1963 to 2006 at the Seedorf camp site (Germany). Upon departure of the Battalion back to the Netherlands, a Fahnenband from the Federal Republic of Germany was attached to the Bataljonsflag. The current modern 42 PainfBat is an operational unit that is part of 13 Light Brigade. The unit has taken over the old traditions of the Regiment Infanterie Limburgse Jagers and is now stationed in the Gen. Maj. De Ruyter van Steveninck barracks in Oirschot.

Baretembleem 19 Regiment Infanterie Oranje Gelderland

 


The Netherlands "Regiment Infanterie Oranje Gelderland"

The Regiment Infanterie Oranje Gelderland is a mechanized infantry regiment of the Royal Netherlands Army. Re-established in 2006 it forms the mechanized infantry battalion 45 Painfbat RIOG. It operates the newly acquired CV90 Infantry fighting vehicle.
In the past they used the DAF YP408: http://www.dafyp408.nl/

see: wikipedia Regiment Infanterie Oranje Gelderland or their website: http://www.45painfbat.nl/
