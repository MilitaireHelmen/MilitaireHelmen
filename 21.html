Germany Bundeswehr beret Sanitätsdienst

Germany Bundeswehr beret Sanitätsdienst
 
Sanitätsdienst = medical corps
 
https://www.sanitaetsdienst-bundeswehr.de/portal/a/sanitaetsdienst/start
 
https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentraler_Sanit%C3%A4tsdienst_der_Bundeswehr
 
The Joint Medical Service (German: Zentraler Sanitätsdienst der Bundeswehr) is a part of the Bundeswehr, the armed forces of Germany and serves all three armed services (Army, Navy and Air Force). However, members of the central medical corps remain members of their respective military branches. Only a few specialized medical units such as the medical care for divers and aircraft crews are not incorporated in the Joint Medical Service. Prior to 2002 each military branch had its own medical service. The services were then largely merged, forming the Joint Medical Service.

Canadian Helmet MK I GSW 1942


These pictures show another Canadian Helmet. It's the same as the British MK II. I know this helmet is made in Canada in 1942 by the letters stamped into the metal. GSW = General Steel Wares, the manufacturer from Toronto Ontario in Canada. You can see the MK I stamped below the GSW. The letter A I is the lot code.
The liner in this helmet is not original, it is a post-war dutch liner.
After the war this helmet is re-furbished and re-painted for use by the Dutch Bescherming Bevolking. (Civil Defense)

This one is most likely used by the BB Medical Service, more about these helmets used by the BB see:
https://dutchhelmets.nl/nederland-netherlands-dutch/lbd-bb-bzb-helm-helmen-arp-cd-helmet-helmets/bescherming-bevolking-civil-defense.html

The Seebataillon is an association of the navy of the Bundeswehr in Schleswig-Holstein. It forms together with the command special forces of the navy the marine infantry. The Seebataillon is stationed in the Preußer barracks in Eckernförde and is subordinate to the Einsatzflottille 1 in Kiel. With parts of the association in the naval base Eckernförde and the Hugo Junkers barracks in Old Duvenstedt is housed.
The soldiers of the naval battalion wear the navy beret with crossed carabiners in gold as a headgear.

Germany beret Wachbataillon

Germany beret Wachbataillon
 
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wachbataillon
 
https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wachbataillon
 
The Wachbataillon (full name: Wachbataillon beim Bundesministerium der Verteidigung (WachBtl BMVg) (Guard battalion at the Federal Ministry of Defence)) is the German Bundeswehr's elite drill unit. The Wachbataillon is the largest battalion of the German forces with about 1,000 soldiers in Berlin. It consists of seven active companies (see list below) and belongs to the Streitkräftebasis (Joint Service Support Command) of the Bundeswehr. The soldiers of the Wachbataillon often refer to themselves as Protter or Protokollsoldaten, meaning protocol soldiers.

Germany beret Topographietruppe

Germany beret Topographietruppe
 
https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topographietruppe
The topographical force was a branch of the Army of the Bundeswehr. Technically, the topographical force was part of the Joint Military Geographic Service. Main task was the procurement and preparation of information about terrain and space and the appropriate supply of the troops with the obtained geo information.
The topographical group was dissolved until 2004. Personnel and equipment were used in the Armed Forces Base to set up the Geoinformation Service of the Bundeswehr. Most army uniform carriers in the geoinformation of the Bundeswehr continue to bear beret, military grade badge and gun color of decommissioned topographical force. In this Bundeswehr Military Geographic Service, most notably in the Bundeswehr's Geoinformation Center, most of the topographical work is continued.

Germany beret Panzeraufklärungstruppe und Feldnachrichtentruppe

Germany beret Panzeraufklärungstruppe und Feldnachrichtentruppe
 
https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panzeraufkl%C3%A4rungstruppe_(Bundeswehr)
The tank reconnaissance troop was a branch of troops in the army of the Bundeswehr. The main task of the tank reconnaissance was reconnaissance and exploration of unknown terrain. To do so the Panzeraufklärungtruppe had reconnaissance and battle tanks. The Panzeraufklärung troop was one of the armored troops and was thus part of the combat troops.
The tank reconnaissance troop was dissolved in 2008. Personnel and equipment were mostly used to set up the army reconnaissance troop.
 
https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feldnachrichtentruppe
The Feldnachrichtentruppe was a branch of troops in the Army of the Bundeswehr. She was an element of the reconnaissance in the army. Task was the non-intelligence information acquisition by questioning and observation of persons. The field intelligence group was one of the army's deployment and leadership support troops.
The field message group was initially named a front-end message group when it was created. In 2008, the field message group was dissolved as a separate branch of service.

Germany Beret Feldjäger

Germany Beret Feldjäger
 
https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feldj%C3%A4gertruppe_(Bundeswehr)
The Feldjägertruppe is the military police of the Bundeswehr and one of the branches of the Streitkräftebasis (SKB) (until 2002: the army).
The Feldjäge are on the one hand a historical type of service and on the other a current branch of the Bundeswehr. In the latter sense, they are under the name Feldjägertruppe since 1955 entrusted with the function of military police in the Bundeswehr. https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feldj%C3%A4ger
 
The Streitkräftebasis  (SKB) was set up in October 2000 as an independent military organization area of the Bundeswehr; However, she is not a separate force. It presents itself as a joint service facility of the Bundeswehr and bundles tasks that were previously carried out by the armed forces (army, air force and navy) on their own responsibility.
