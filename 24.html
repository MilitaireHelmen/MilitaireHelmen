Belgian Beret Field Accommodation Unit

Belgian Beret Field Accommodation Unit
 
https://www.mil.be/nl/eenheden/field-accommodation-unit
The Field Accommodation Unit (FAU) is located at Beauvechain airbase and has eighty personnel.
The unit is responsible for the infrastructure of the detachments during operations. This includes the construction and maintenance of the accommodation (heated and air-conditioned tents and containers), offices, kitchens, sanitary facilities, power supplies, water purification, and so on.
The unit also provides specialized equipment to the Air Component, such as aircraft refueling facilities or runway lighting systems. She also helps the Medical Component set up her facilities.
 
The FAU's motto is: Muniendum saepe, semper auxiliandum (Often building, always giving support).

Beret The Netherlands "Marinier-Commando"

Beret The Netherlands "Marinier-Commando"
 
Previously, it was possible for members of the Marine Corps to complete the Dutch Commando training. He was presented with his green beret with red blood patch and anchor after sucsesfully completing the Elementary Command Training (ECO) training. 
https://www.defensie.nl/onderwerpen/historische-canons/historische-canon-korps-commando-troepen/de-commando/de-elementaire-commando-opleiding
 
https://www.mariniercommando.nl/
https://www.mariniercommando.nl/foto-s-marinier-commando/
 
Elementary Command Training (ECO)
Commands speak to the imagination: soldiers who carry out very heavy missions. They are soldiers who want to explore and push the limits of their ability. The Korps Commandotroepen (KCT) selects candidate commands based on those character traits. The ultimate test is the illustrious Elementary Command Training (ECO).
 
ECO elements
Well-known elements from the ECO are the survival week in the Biesbosch and the climbing week in the Belgian Ardennes. After 7 weeks, the climax follows: the so-called "weary". In that last week, the aspirants have to cover more than 200 kilometers, mostly on foot.
There are physical assignments between different stages. During the last night, they cover the 25-kilometer "commander's march" with a heavier load. This tour ends in the Engelbrecht of Nassau barracks in Roosendaal. Here they come in through the "Tears Gate" on Friday morning. The ECO is then over. Whoever has made it, can exchange the hat tie for a (beer-filled) green beret during a special roll call.
 
Preparation
The ECO is a selection phase in the process of becoming a command. That selection is not tender. The prospective commandos undergo rigorous training for 8 weeks under difficult physical and mental conditions. It is the intention that only the best remain.
The student dinner is the last decent meal. Then the ECO starts with the so-called "hat tie exercise". During this roll call, students will perform with 2 typical historical pieces of equipment: the hat and the toggle rope. In this way they wear the uniform of the first Dutch commandos during the Second World War in the Commando Basic Training. They got this outfit from the British in the Scottish town of Achnacarry.

The Netherlands beret DBBO

The Netherlands beret DBBO
 
DBBO = Defensie Bewakings- en Beveiligingsorganisatie = Defense Security and Security Organization
 
https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defensie_Bewakings-_en_BeveiligingsOrganisatie
The Defense Security and Security Organization (DBBO) is part of the Dutch armed forces. The DBBO was established on January 1, 2011. This organization is a so-called 24-hour company (full continuous operation) and falls under the Defense Support Command.
Before the arrival of the DBBO, the Defense units themselves were responsible for the security and surveillance of their objects. By placing the surveillance and security tasks within 1 central organization, a uniform working method is used throughout the Defense organization. The occasion for the establishment of the DBBO is the Decision-making Memorandum (BVM) 2008 measure 1-10, in which a start was made to make all the security and security tasks within Defense into one organization.
The DBBO is responsible for the execution of the security and security duties within the Ministry of Defense (National Act on the Use of Violence by Guards of Military Objects). As a second main task, the DBBO advises on organizational, structural and electronic (OBE) security measures for the entire Defense organization.
