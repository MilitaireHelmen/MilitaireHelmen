The Netherlands M53 helmet 1955 Police (part 3)

The Netherlands M53 helmet 1955 Police (part 3)
Continued frpm Part 2
 
Pictures with details:
 
First picture shows the inscriptions within the helmet shell: VB555B3
VB = Verblifa, manufacturer of the shell
55 =  1955 year the shell was made.
 
https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verblifa
https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomassen_%26_Drijver-Verblifa
 

 
The liner shows severel stamps.
 
SW 65  6
SW = Schuberth Werke = the manufacturer of the Liner
65 is the year of production 1965
6 = June
The N is the material used to make the liner.

D.v.D = Departement van Defensie
D.K.G. = Dienst Kwartiermeester Generaal 
see https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Materieellogistiek_Commando


 
I found a picture showing Po;ice members wearing the M53 helmet:
picture from:
https://www.politieacademie.nl/kennisenonderzoek/kennis/mediatheek/PDF/87493.PDF

The Netherlands Beret "Opleidingscentrum voor Initiële Opleidingen"

The Netherlands Beret "Opleidingscentrum voor Initiële Opleidingen"
"OCIO"
 
For most of the new recruits this is the first beret they will wear.
At the OCIO they learn all about their live in the Military.
After their initial training at the OCIO the recruits will go to the "Functionele Opleiding" where they learn the skills for the job they will full fill in the army.

more information: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opleidingscentrum_voor_Initi%C3%ABle_Opleidingen
 
The inside is removed so I do not know the age and the manufacturer of this beret.
This beret model designated DT2000 is the currently used beret.

The Netherlands Beret Regiment Geneeskundige Troepen (yellow)

 
Beret The Netherlands Regiment Geneeskundige Troepen
= Regiment Medical Troops
 
Motto:
"Eripiendo Victoriae Prosum"  = While helping, I serve the victory
 
In 2001 a new beret and emblem with yellow background  was introduced.
In 2010 the yellow background was replaced again by a green background.
 
Beret is made by the company Hassing Amersfoort
Hassing BV
NSN 8405-17-117-5515
O.nr.872.2665.1911.11.05
Maat 56 = Size 56

Belgian beret 12 Linie

Belgian beret 12 Linie =  Infantry Regiment
 
A.B.L. = Armée Belge / Belgisch Leger
= Army Belgium
 
Beret made by preta in the year 1982.
PRETA according to Google still exists as manufacturer of professional clothing.
"Pre"vost-"Ta"vernier BVBA;
Oude Tieltstraat 118 A Meulebeke .
 
The 12th Linie was an Infantry Regiment.
Since 1993 it has been combined with the  13th Linie Regiment.
