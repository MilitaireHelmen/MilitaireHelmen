In the event of a fire in an empty hall on the Munster military training area (Heidekreis) in the early Sunday morning, the municipal fire brigade and the Bundeswehr fire brigade worked together to fight the fire. A Bundeswehr armored personnel carrier was also used. The tank tore down parts of the building to enable better fire fighting. The extinguishing water supply could be ensured with over 30 B-lengths and shuttle traffic. Photo: Heidekreis Fire Brigade / Stephan Meier (Image: S. Meier)

Armed Forces Fire Department? What's this?
The Bundeswehr fire brigade forms the central fire service facility of the German armed forces. In addition to professional fire brigades, volunteer fire brigades, company fire brigades, compulsory fire brigades, ... the Bundeswehr fire brigade forms its own type of fire brigade due to its membership in the military. Around 72 different units are spread across all Bundeswehr locations in Germany and abroad. With a strength of more than 3,050 forces, the Bundeswehr fire brigade would be the second largest professional fire brigade in Germany after BF Berlin. In spite of the special position, all of the BW fire brigades are professional firefighters, which also means that the training of the forces is recognized as civil.
 
What are the tasks of the Bundeswehr fire department?
Basically, the Bundeswehr fire brigade takes over all tasks in the field of fire protection and danger prevention at the respective military location. The following applies: Just as specialized as the Bundeswehr is through its sub-armed forces, the fire brigade must be set up just as specialized. At naval locations, for example, there are units that specialize in ship and submarine fire fighting.
At the same time, Bundeswehr fire departments at air force facilities perform the same tasks as a civil airport fire service. Fire protection units on military training areas take care of wildfires. The peculiarity: at all facilities of the Bundeswehr there is an increased risk of explosive substances such as ammunition, weapons and fuels. This fact alone makes a fire brigade necessary, which is adapted to the respective local conditions through its equipment and training.
 
Are Bundeswehr firefighters soldiers?
It goes without saying that the Bundeswehr has its own fire brigade for reasons of confidentiality in the military alone. One reason why most Bundeswehr firefighters are soldiers. With the realignment of the Bundeswehr in 2012, civilian employees were also brought into the ranks of the Bundeswehr fire service. They are already an existential part of the group.
Interested parties usually start their careers as firefighters (m / f) in the medium-sized fire service. Uses as officials in the higher and higher fire service are also possible. A career as a firefighter in the Bundeswehr can also be pursued in the military career (non-commissioned officer, officer).
 
The Bundeswehr fire department
The Bundeswehr fire brigade positions itself as the second largest professional fire brigade in Germany - behind the Berlin fire brigade. She has very special guards. Your tasks range from area to aircraft fire fighting.
We took a look behind the scenes at the Bundeswehr Fire Protection Center in Sonthofen (BY) and introduced the new structure.

Germany beret Fernspähtruppe

Germany beret Fernspähtruppe
 
https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernsp%C3%A4htruppe_(Bundeswehr)
 
The Fernspähtruppe was a type of troop in the German army. The remote scouts were among the Bundeswehr's operational and command support troops. The main mission was to gain key information deep behind the enemy lines through small, self-sufficient, long-distance spy teams operating on foot.
The Fernspähtuppe was decommissioned in 2008. The majority of the personnel was used to deploy the Army Reconnaissance Troop. Smaller parts switched to the special forces. The special forces and the army reconnaissance troops continue to deploy sub-units that operate similarly to the disbanded Fernspah troop.

The Netherlands Beret Militair-Psychologische en Sociologische Dienst
 
https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Militair-Psychologische_en_Sociologische_Dienst
The Military Psychological and Sociological Service (usually abbreviated to MPSD) is one of the service subjects of the Royal Netherlands Army.
Psychologists and sociologists have been members of the armed forces since the 1920s. They were initially used primarily for selection, later also for advice, education and training, research and (psychotherapeutic) treatments. In the 1970s their number increased to a few dozen. This led in 1973 to the creation of a special service section: the MPSD. The members of this department are all officers of the Royal Netherlands Army and graduated (drs. Or now masters) in psychology, sociology or educational sciences.

Belgian Beret "3 Chasseurs à Cheval /  3de Jagers te Paard"
 
3rd Hunters on Horse
 
In January 1918 the 3e Lansiers is split into two groups. The first group becomes the Light cavalry Group of the 1st Army Division, the second group as the Light cavalry Group of the 2nd Army Division.
In January 1920, the second group was renamed 3rd Hunters on Horse Regiment. On February 10, 1926, the regiment was dissolved and was no longer re-established during the 1939 mobilization.
On March 13, 1952 the Regiment was brought back to life as a reconnaissance unit to be abolished again in 1960.
In 1969 the 3rd Hunters on Horse becomes the "Battallion Léger" of the province of Hainaut.

Belgian Beret "Batterij ParaCommando"

Belgian Beret "Batterij ParaCommando"
 
The Para-Commando Battery is one of the support units of the ParaCommandos. The unit is in Dutch and wears the green beret. Since March 2, 2004 'de Battery' has been part of 2A and has the same structure as a classic battery field artillery.
 
The annual program of the Battery Para-Commando is abundantly filled with both infantry and artillery exercises. However, the new equipment and professionalism of the soldiers and the framework make it possible for these two missions to be fulfilled properly and with pride.
