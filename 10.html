South Africa MKII helmet 3 (part 2)

 Continued from South Africa MKII helmet 3 part 1
 
This helmet came with a leather chinstrap.
I found information that after the war many South African helmets ended up in Greece, I think it is possible this helmet was used by Greece and the leather chinstrap is Greece.
 
Most of the green paint on this helmet is gone and the original sand color can be seen again.
 
The liner of this helmet is English it shows CCL 11 1945.
CCL = Charles Collett Ltd.
 
At a certain moment South Africa started to use English liners.
Greece also manufactured their own liners but I do not have one yet in my collection.
 

http://brendonshelmets.weebly.com/greece-mkii.html

French Helmet "Casque Troupes Toutes Armes Modèle 51 OTAN" Japy 1953

French Helmet "Casque Troupes Toutes Armes Modèle 51 OTAN" Japy 1953
 
French Helmet "Casque Troupes Toutes Armes Modèle 51 OTAN" or "TTA 51"
After world war 2 the French came up with their own US M1 clone.
It looks a lot like the US M1 but the shape of the shell is different. The US M1 liner will not fit into the TTA 51.
This TTA 51 is a type 2, it has swivel bails to hold the chin strap. The type 1 has fixed bails like the first US M1 helmets.
My helmet is made in 1953 by Japy
 For pictures of the liner that came with this helmet see: part 2

French Helmet Sous Casque radio char M65 Galland

French Helmet Sous Casque radio char M65 Galland
 
This is a French Helmet "Sous Casque radio char M65"
Sous Casque means under helmet or liner. and could be used as a liner for the previous presented Model 51 TTA or the Model 56 TAP.
Radio Char means tank radio, this helmet was designed so it could be used together with a tank communication system.
This helmet is made by Galland in 1966.
The R stamped in the mitlle tells us it is made of Rilsan.
The interior of this helmet is made just like the US M1 liner.
For lots of additional information visit:
http://www.world-war-helmets.com/fiche?q=Casque-Francais-Tankiste-Mle-65

Belgium Beret Logistiek

Belgium Beret Logistiek
 
A.B.L. = Armée Belge / Belgisch Leger
= Army Belgium
 
Blue Beret made by preta in the year 1989.
PRETA according to Google still exists as manufacturer of professional clothing.
"Pre"vost-"Ta"vernier BVBA;
Oude Tieltstraat 118 A Meulebeke .
 
From this forum https://www.ablhistoryforum.be/viewtopic.php?f=39&t=819&p=49819#p49819
I have learned all Logistics Bataljons are wearing the same beret and emblem.
The Blue beret can have this insignia in 3 different colors:
copper = troops
silver    = nco's non commissioned officers (onderofficieren)
gold = officers
 
mitin is the product they used to protect the fabric against the moth.
 


 
The 18 Battalion Logistics is a logistics unit based on Leopoldsburg.
The unit has almost 450 military personnel.
It distributes food and equipment to the units, transports equipment and installs mobile sanitary installations.
The tasks of the battalion are:
-To ensure logistical support during operations at home and abroad
-Provide logistical support to the units
-To defend territory and national interests
-Participating in relief operations in the event of disasters or catastrophes
-Provide general territorial support in Belgium.
 
The motto of the unit is Pugnantes adjuvo (I help the fighters).
 
https://www.mil.be/fr/unites/18-bataillon-logistique
Le 18 Bataillon Logistique est une unité logistique basée à Bourg-Léopold et comptant près de 450 militaires.
 
Elle s'occupe entre autres, de l'approvisionnement en nourriture et en équipement, du transport de matériel ou encore de l'installation de sanitaires mobiles.
Ses missions consistent à :
Participer aux opérations dans un cadre national et international avec pour tâche première d'assurer l'appui logistique ;Fournir un appui logistique à toutes les unités ;Prendre part à la défense du territoire ou des intérêts de la Nation ;Assurer l'aide à la Nation ;Contribuer à fournir l'appui territorial général en Belgique.La devise de l'unité est « Pugnantes adjuvo » et signifie «  j'aide les combattants ».
 
https://www.mil.be/nl/eenheden/18-bataljon-logistiek
Het 18 Bataljon Logistiek is een logistieke eenheid met als basis Leopoldsburg. De eenheid telt bijna 450 militairen.
Ze verdeelt onder andere voeding en uitrusting aan de eenheden, transporteert materieel en installeert mobiele sanitaire installaties.
De opdrachten van het bataljon zijn:
de logistieke steun verzekeren tijdens operaties in binnen- en buitenlandde logistieke steun verzorgen aan de eenhedenhet grondgebied en de nationale belangen helpen te verdedigendeelnemen aan hulpacties bij rampen of catastrofesalgemene territoriale steun leveren in België.De wapenspreuk van de eenheid is Pugnantes adjuvo (Ik help de strijders).
